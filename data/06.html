34. The harmonic oscillator　調和振動子

A SIMPLE and interesting example of a dynamical system in quantum mechanics is the harmonic oscillator. This example is of importance for general theory, because it forms a corner-stone in the theory of radiation. The dynamical variables needed for describing the system are just one coordinate \(q\) and its conjugate momentum \(p\). The Hamiltonian in classical mechanics is
量子力学における力学系の単純かつ興味深い例として、調和振動子が挙げられます。この例は、放射理論の基礎となるため、一般理論にとって重要です。この系を記述するために必要な力学変数は、座標 \(q\) とその共役運動量 \(p\) だけです。古典力学におけるハミルトニアンは、 \[ H = \frac{1}{2m}(p^2+m^2\omega^2q^2) \tag{1} \] where \(m\) is the mass of the oscillating particle and \(\omega\) is \(2\pi\) times the frequency. We assume the same Hamiltonian in quantum mechanics. This Hamiltonian, together with the quantum condition (10) of §22, define the system completely.
ここで、\(m\) は振動粒子の質量、\(\omega\) は振動数の \(2\pi\) 倍である。量子力学においても同じハミルトニアンを仮定する。このハミルトニアンは、§22の量子条件(10)と共に、系を完全に定義する。

The Heisenberg equations of motion are
ハイゼンベルクの運動方程式は \[ \left. \begin{align} \dot{q}_t &= [q_t,H] = p_t/m \\ \\ \dot{p}_t &= [p_t,H] = -m\omega^2q_t \end{align} \right\} \tag {2} \] It is convenient to introduce the dimensionless complex dynamical variable
無次元複素力学変数を導入すると便利である。 \[ \eta = (2m\hbar\omega)^{-\frac{1}{2}}(p+im\omega q) \tag{3} \] The equations of motion (2) give

この式を積分すると次の式が得られます。 \[ \eta_t = \eta_0e^{i\omega t} \tag{4} \] where \(\eta_0\) is a linear operator independent of \(t\), and is equal to the value of \(\eta_t\) at time \(t= 0\). The above equations are all as in the classical theory.
ここで、\(\eta_0\) は \(t\) に依存しない線型作用素であり、時刻 \(t= 0\) における \(\eta_t\) の値に等しい。上記の式はすべて古典理論におけるものと同じである。

同様に \[ \hbar\omega\overline{\eta}\eta = H + \frac{1}{2}\hbar\omega \tag{6} \] Thus
したがって \[ \overline{\eta}\eta - \eta\overline{\eta} = 1 \tag{7} \] Equation (5) or (6) gives \(H\) in terms of \(\eta\) and \(\overline{\eta}\) and (7) gives the commutation relation connecting \(\eta\) and \(\overline{\eta}\). From (5)
式(5)または式(6)は \(\eta\) と \(\overline{\eta}\) を用いて \(H\) を与え、式(7)は \(\eta\) と \(\overline{\eta}\) を結ぶ交換関係を与える。(5)より \[ \hbar\omega\overline{\eta}\eta\overline{\eta} = \overline{\eta}H - \frac{1}{2}\hbar\omega\overline{\eta} \] and from (6)
そして(6)から \[ \hbar\omega\overline{\eta}\eta\overline{\eta} = H\overline{\eta}+\frac{1}{2}\hbar\omega\overline{\eta} \] Thus

Let \(|0\rangle\) be an eigenket of \(H\) belonging to the lowest eigenvalue \(\frac{1}{2}\hbar\omega\), so that
\(|0\rangle\) を \(H\) の固有ケットとし、最低の固有値 \(\frac{1}{2}\hbar\omega\) に属するものとすると、 \[ \overline{\eta}|0\rangle = 0 \tag{13} \] and form the sequence of kets
そしてケット列を形成する \[ |0\rangle,　\eta|0\rangle,　\eta^2|0\rangle,　\eta^3|0\rangle,　\cdots \tag{14} \] These kets are all eigenkets of \(H\), belonging to the sequence of eigen- values (12) respectively. From (9) and (13)
これらのケットはすべて\(H\)の固有ケットであり、それぞれ固有値の列(12)に属する。(9)と(13)より \[ \overline{\eta}\eta^n|0\rangle = n\eta^{n-1}|0\rangle \tag{15} \] for any non-negative integer \(n\). Thus the set of kets (14) is such that \(\eta\) or \(\overline{\eta}\) applied to any one of the set gives a ket dependent on the set. Now all the dynamical variables in our problem are expressible in terms of \(\eta\) and \(\overline{\eta}\), so the kets (14) must form a complete set (otherwise there would be some more dynamical variables). There is just one of these kets for each eigenvalue (12) of \(H\), so \(H\) by itself forms a complete commuting set of observables. The kets (14) correspond to the various stationary states of the oscillator. The stationary state with energy \((n+\frac{1}{2})\hbar\omega\), corresponding to \(\eta^n|0\rangle\), is called the \(n\)-th quantum state.
任意の非負整数 \(n\) に対して成り立つ。したがって、ケットの集合 (14) は、\(\eta\) または \(\overline{\eta}\) を集合のいずれにも適用すると、その集合に依存するケットが生成される。 これで、問題におけるすべての動的変数は \(\eta\) と \(\overline{\eta}\) で表現できるため、ケット (14) は完全な集合を形成する必要がある（そうでなければ、さらに動的変数が存在する）。\(H\) の各固有値 (12) に対してこれらのケットが 1 つだけ存在するため、\(H\) 自体は観測可能な値の完全な可換集合を形成する。ケット (14) は振動子のさまざまな定常状態に対応する。エネルギーが \((n+\frac{1}{2})\hbar\omega\) である定常状態 (\(\eta^n|0\rangle\) に対応) は、\(n\) 番目の量子状態と呼ばれます。

(15)の助けを借りて、帰納的に次の式が得られる。 \[ \langle 0|{\overline{\eta}}^n\eta^n|0\rangle = n! \tag{16} \] provided \(|0\rangle\) is normalized. Thus the kets (14) multiplied by the coefficients \(n!^{-\frac{1}{2}}\) with \(n = 0,1, 2,...,\) respectively form the basic kets of a representation, namely the representation with H diagonal. Any ket \(|x\rangle\) can be expanded in the form
ただし、\(|0\rangle\) は正規化されている。したがって、ケット (14) に係数 \(n!^{-\frac{1}{2}}\)（\(n = 0,1, 2,...,\)）を乗じたものは、表現の基本ケット、すなわち H 対角を持つ表現を形成する。任意のケット \(|x\rangle\) は、次のように展開できる。 \[ |x\rangle = \sum_0^\infty x_n \eta^n|0\rangle \tag{17} \] where the \(x_n\)'s are numbers. In this way the ket \(|x\rangle\) is put into correspondence with a power series \(\sum x_n\eta^n\) in the variable \(\eta\), the various terms in the power series corresponding to the various stationary states. If \(|x\rangle\) is normalized, it defines a state for which the probability of the oscillator being in the \(n\)-th quantum state, i.e. the probability of \(H\) having the value (\(n+\frac{1}{2})\hbar\omega\), is

We may consider the ket \(|0\rangle\) as a standard ket and the power series in \(\eta\) as a wave function, since any ket can be expressed as such a wave function multiplied into this standard ket. We get a kind of wave function differing from the usual kind, introduced by equations (62) of §20, in that it is a function of the complex dynamical variable \(\eta\) instead of observables. It was first introduced by V. Fock, so we shall call the representation Fock's representation. It is for many purposes the most convenient representation for describing states of the harmonic oscillator. The standard ket \(|0\rangle\) satisfies the condition (13), which replaces the conditions (43) of §22 for the standard ket in Schrödinger's representation.

35. Angular momentum　角運動量

Let us consider a particle described by the three Cartesian coordi- nates \(x, y, z\) and their conjugate momenta \(p_x,p_y,p_z\). Its angular momentum about the origin is defined as in the classical theory, by
3つの直交座標\(x, y, z\)とそれらの共役運動量\(p_x, p_y, p_z\)で記述される粒子を考えてみましょう。その原点周りの角運動量は、古典理論と同様に次のように定義されます。 \[ m_x=yp_z - zp_y　　　m_y=zp_x - xp_z　　　m_z=xp_y - yp_x \tag{22} \] or by the vector equation
あるいはベクトル方程式によって \[ \mathbf{m} = \mathbf{x} \times \mathbf{p} \] We must evaluate the P.B.s of the angular momentum components with the dynamical variables \(x, p_x,\) etc., and with each other. This we can do most conveniently with the help of the laws (4) and (5) of §21, thus
角運動量成分のP.B.を、力学変数\(x, p_x,\)などを用いて、また互いに評価する必要がある。これは、§21の法則(4)と(5)を用いて最も簡単に行うことができる。 \[ \left. \begin{align} [m_z,x] &= [xp_y - yp_x, x] = -y[p_x,x] = y \\ \\ [m_z,y] &= [xp_y - yp_x, y] = x[p_y,y] = -x \end{align} \right\} \tag{23} \] \[ [m_z,z] = [xp_y - yp_x, z] = 0 \tag{24} \] and similarly,
同様に、 \[ [m_z, p_x] = p_y,　　　[m_z,p_y] = —p_x \tag{25} \\ \] \[ [m_z,p_z| = 0 \tag{26} \] with corresponding relations for \(m_x\) and \(m_y\). Again
\(m_x\)と\(m_y\)の関係も同様である。 \[ [m_y, m_z] = [zp_x - xp_z, m_z] = z[p_x,m_z] - [x,m_z]p_z \] \[ \left. \begin{align} &=-zp_y + yp_z = m_x \\ \\ [m_z, m_x] &= m_y,　　[m_x,m_y] = m_z \end{align} \right\} \tag{27} \] These results are all the same as in the classical theory. The sign in the results (23), (25), and (27) may easily be remembered from the rule that the \(+\) sign occurs when the three dynamical! variables, con- sisting of the two in the P.B. on the left-hand side'and the one forming the result on the right, are in the cyclic order (xyz) and the \(—\) sign occurs otherwise. Equations (27) may be put in the vector form
これらの結果はすべて古典理論の場合と同じである。結果(23)、(25)、(27)の符号は、左辺のP.B.の2つと右辺の結果を形成する1つの変数からなる3つの動的変数が巡回順序(xyz)にあるときに\(+\)符号が付き、そうでないときに\(—\)符号が付くという規則から容易に覚えられる。式(27)はベクトル形式で表すことができる。 \[ \mathbf{m} \times \mathbf{m} = i\hbar \mathbf{m} \tag{28} \]

Now suppose we have several particles with angular momenta \(\mathbf{m}_1,\mathbf{m}_2,...\). Each of these angular momentum vectors will satisfy (28), thus
ここで、角運動量を持つ複数の粒子があるとします \(\mathbf{m}_1,\mathbf{m}_2,...\)。これらの角運動量ベクトルはそれぞれ、(28)式を満たすので、 \[ \mathbf{m}_r \times \mathbf{m}_r = i\hbar\mathbf{m}_r \] and any one of them will commute with any other, so that
そしてそれらのどれか一つが他のどれとも交換可能であり、 \[ \mathbf{m}_r \times \mathbf{m}_s + \mathbf{m}_s \times \mathbf{m}_r = 0\;(r \neq s) \]

Hence if \(\mathbf{M} = \sum\limits_r \mathbf{m}_r\) is the total angular momentum,
したがって、\(\mathbf{M} = \sum\limits_r \mathbf{m}_r\) が全角運動量である場合、 \[ \begin{align} \mathbf{M} \times \mathbf{M} &= \sum_{rs} \mathbf{m}_r \times \mathbf{m}_s =\sum_r \mathbf{m}_r \times \mathbf{m}_r + \sum_{r \lt s} (\mathbf{m}_r \times \mathbf{m}_s + \mathbf{m}_s \times \mathbf{m}_r) \\ \\ &= i\hbar \sum_r \mathbf{m}_r = i\hbar\mathbf{M} \tag{29} \end{align} \] This result is of the same form as (28), so that the components of the total angular momentum \(\mathbf{M}\) of any number of particles satisfy the same commutation relations as those of the angular momentum of a single particle.

The above argument applies to the angular momentum arising from the motion of particles, defined by (22) for each particle. There is another kind of angular momentum occurring in atomic theory, spin angular momentum. The former kind of angular momentum will be called orbital angular momentum, to distinguish it. The spin angu- lar momentum of a particle should be pictured as due to some internal motion of the particle, so that it is associated with different degrees of freedom from those describing the motion of the particle as a whole, and hence the dynamical variables that describe the spin must com- mute with \(x,y,z,p_x,p_y,\) and \(p_z\). The spin does not correspond very closely to anything in classical mechanics, so the method of classical analogy is not suitable for studying it. However, we can build up a theory of the spin simply from the assumption that the components of the spin angular momentum are connected with the rotation opera- tors in the same way as we had above for orbital angular momentum, i.e. equation (32) holds with \(M_z\) as the \(z\) component of the spin angular momentum of a particle and \(r_z\) as the rotation operator about the \(z\)-axis referring to states of spin of that particle. With this assumption, the commutation relations connecting the components of the spin angular momentum \(\mathbf{M}\) with any vector \(\mathbf{A}\) referring to the spin must be of the standard form (30), and hence, taking \(\mathbf{A}\) to be the spin angular momentum itself, we have equation (29) holding also for the spin. We now have (29) holding quite generally, for any sum of spin and orbital angular momenta, and also (30) will hold generally, for \(\mathbf{M}\) the total spin and orbital angular momentum and \(\mathbf{A}\) any vector dynamical variable, and the connexion between angular momentum and rotation operators will be always valid.

For an isolated system, the Hamiltonian must be unchanged by any rotation about the origin, in other words it must be a scalar, so it must commute with the angular momentum about the origin. Thus the angular momentum is a constant of the motion. For this argument the origin may be any point.

For a dynamical system involving other dynamical variables besides \(m_x,m_y,\) and \(m_z\), there may be variables that do not commute with \(\beta\). Then \(\beta\) is no longer a number, but a general linear operator. This happens for any orbital angular momentum (22), as \(x, y, z, p_x, p_y,\) and \(p_z\) do not commute with \(\beta\). We shall assume that \(\beta\) is always an observable, and \(k\) can then be defined by (39) with the positive square root function and is also an observable. We shall call \(k\) so defined the magnitude of the angular momentum vector \(\mathbf{m}\) in the general case. The above analysis by which we obtained the eigenvalues of \(m_z\) is still valid if we replace \(|m_z\rangle\) by a simultaneous eigenket \(|k^\prime m_z^\prime\rangle\) of the commuting observables \(k\) and \(m_z\), and leads to the result that the possible eigenvalues for \(k\) are the numbers (45), and for each eigenvalue \(k^\prime\) of \(k\) the eigenvalues of \(m_z\) are the numbers (42) with \(k^\prime\) substituted for \(k\). We have here an example of a phenomenon which we have not met with previously, namely that with two commuting observables, the eigenvalues of one depend on what eigenvalue we assign to the other. This phenomenon may be understood as the two observables being not altogether independent, but partially functions of one another. The number of independent simultaneous eigenkets of \(k\) and \(m_z\) belonging to the eigenvalues \(k^\prime\) and \(m_z^\prime\) must be indepen- dent of \(m_z^\prime\), since for each independent \(|k^\prime m_z^prime\rangle\) we can obtain an independent \(|k^\prime m_z^{\prime\prime}\rangle\), for any \(m_z^{\prime\prime}\) in the sequence (42), by multiplying \(k^\prime m_z^\prime\rangle\) by a suitable power of \(\eta\) or \(\overline{|\eta}\).

38. Motion in a central field of force　中心力場における運動

An atom consists of a massive positively charged nucleus together with a number of electrons moving round, under the influence of the attractive force of the nucleus and their own mutual repulsions. An exact treatment of this dynamical system is a very difficult mathe- matical problem. One can, however, gain some insight into the main features of the system by making the rough approximation of regard- ing each electron as moving independently in a certain cenéral field of force, namely that of the nucleus, assumed fixed, together with some kind of average of the forces due to the other electrons. Thus our present problem of the motion of a particle in a central field of force forms a corner-stone in the theory of the atom.

40. Selection rules　選択ルール

If a dynamical system is set up in a certain stationary state, it will remain in that stationary state so long as it is not acted upon by outside forces. Any atomic system in practice, however, frequently gets acted upon by external electromagnetic fields, under whose influence it is liable to cease to be in one stationary state and to make a transition to another. The theory of such transitions will be de- veloped in §44 and §45. A result of this theory is that, to a high degree of accuracy, transitions between two states cannot occur under the influence of electromagnetic radiation if, in a Heisenberg representa- tion with these two stationary states as two of the basic states, the matrix element, referring to these two states, of the representative of the total electric displacement \(D\) of the system vanishes. Now it happens for many atomic systems that the great majority of the matrix elements of \(D\) in a Heisenberg representation do vanish, and hence there are severe limitations on the possibilities for transitions. The rules that express these limitations are called selection rules.
力学系がある定常状態に設定されると、外部からの力が作用しない限り、その定常状態を維持する。しかしながら、実際には、いかなる原子系も外部電磁場の作用を頻繁に受け、その影響下では、ある定常状態を脱して別の定常状態へと遷移する傾向がある。このような遷移の理論は、§44および§45で展開される。この理論の結果として、これらの2つの定常状態を基本状態の2つとするハイゼンベルク表示において、系の全電気変位 \(D\) を表す行列要素（これらの2つの状態を参照する）がゼロとなる場合、高い精度で、2つの状態間の遷移は電磁放射の影響下では発生しない。さて、多くの原子系では、ハイゼンベルク表現における \(D\) の行列要素の大部分が消滅し、したがって遷移の可能性には厳しい制限が課せられます。 これらの制限を表す規則は選択規則と呼ばれます。

磁場がそれほど大きくない場合は、\(\mathscr{H}^2\)を含む項を無視することができるので、ハミルトニアン(88)は次のように簡約される。 \[ \begin{align} H &= \frac{1}{2m}(p_x^2+p_y^2+p_z^2)-\frac{e^2}{r}+\frac{e\mathscr{H}}{2mc}(xp_y-yp_x)+\frac{e\hbar\mathscr{H}}{2mc}\sigma_z \\ \\ &=\frac{1}{2m}(p_x^2+p_y^2+p_z^2)-\frac{e^2}{r}+\frac{e\mathscr{H}}{2mc}(m_z+\hbar\sigma_z) \tag{89} \end{align} \] The extra terms due to the magnetic field are now \(e\mathscr{H}/2mc.(m_z+\hbar\sigma_z)\). But these extra terms commute with the total Hamiltonian and are thus constants of the motion. This makes the problem very easy. The stationary states of the system, i.e. the eigenstates of the Hamil- tonian (89), will be those eigenstates of the Hamiltonian for no field that are simultaneously eigenstates of the observables \(m_z\) and \(\sigma_z\), or at least of the one observable \(m_+\hbar\sigma_z\) and the energy-levels of the system will be those for the system with no field, given by (80) if one considers only closed states, increased by an eigenvalue of \(e\mathscr{H}/2mc.(m_z+\hbar\sigma_z)\). Thus stationary states of the system with no field for which \(m_z\) has the numerical value \(m_z^\prime\), an integral multiple of \(\hbar\), and for which also \(\sigma_z\) has the numerical value \(\sigma_z^\prime =\pm 1\), will still be stationary states when the field is applied. Their energy will be increased by an amount consisting of the sum of two parts, a part \(e\mathscr{H}/2mc.m_z^\prime\) arising from the orbital motion, which part may be con- sidered as due to an orbital magnetic moment \(-em_z^\prime/2mc\), and a part \(e\mathscr{H}/2mc.h\sigma_z^\prime\) arising from the spin. The ratio of the orbital magnetic moment to the orbital angular momentum \(m_z^\prime\) is \(-e/2mc\), which is half the ratio of the spin magnetic moment to the spin angular momentum. This fact is sometimes referred to as the magnetic anomaly of the spin.
磁場による余分な項は、\(e\mathscr{H}/2mc.(m_z+\hbar\sigma_z)\) となります。 しかし、これらの余分な項は全ハミルトニアンと交換するため、運動定数となります。これにより、問題は非常に簡単になります。 系の定常状態、すなわちハミルトニアン (89) の固有状態は、場がない場合のハミルトニアンの固有状態のうち、観測量 \(m_z\) と \(\sigma_z\) の固有状態、または少なくとも 1 つの観測量 \(m_+\hbar\sigma_z\) の固有状態であるものとなり、系のエネルギー準位は、閉じた状態のみを考慮すると、場がない場合の系のエネルギー準位 (80) に固有値 \(e\mathscr{H}/2mc.(m_z+\hbar\sigma_z)\) を加えたものとなる。したがって、磁場がかかっていない系の定常状態において、\(m_z\) が数値 \(m_z^\prime\)（\(\hbar\) の整数倍）を持ち、\(\sigma_z\) が数値 \(\sigma_z^\prime =\pm 1\) を持つ場合、磁場を印加しても定常状態のままである。そのエネルギーは、軌道運動に起因する部分（\(e\mathscr{H}/2mc.m_z^\prime\)）と、スピンに起因する部分（\(e\mathscr{H}/2mc.h\sigma_z^\prime\)）の和によって増加する。軌道磁気モーメントと軌道角運動量の比 \(m_z^\prime\) は \(-e/2mc\) であり、これはスピン磁気モーメントとスピン角運動量の比の半分です。この事実は、スピンの磁気異常と呼ばれることもあります。

Since the energy-levels now involve \(m_z\), the selection rule for \(m_z\) obtained in the preceding section becomes capable of direct com- parison with experiment. We take a Heisenberg representation in which, among other constants of the motion, \(m_z\) and \(\sigma_z\) are diagonal. The selection rule for \(m_z\) now requires \(m_z\) to change by \(\hbar\), 0, or \(—\hbar\), while \(\sigma_z\), since it commutes with the electric displacement, will not change at all. Thus the energy difference between the two states taking part in the transition process will differ by an amount \(e\mathscr{H}/2mc\), 0, or \(-e\hbar\mathscr{H}/2mc\) from its value for no magnetic field. Hence, from Bohr's frequency condition, the frequency of the associated electromagnetic radiation will differ by \(e\mathscr{H}/4\pi mc\), 0, or \(-e\mathscr{H}/4\pi mc\) from that for no magnetic field. This means that each spectral line for no magnetic field gets split up by the field into three components. If one considers radiation moving in the \(z\)-direction, then from (83) the two outer components will be circularly polarized, while the central undisplaced one will be of zero intensity. These results are in agreement with experiment and also with the classical theory of the Zeeman effect.
エネルギー準位に \(m_z\) が含まれるようになったため、前節で得られた \(m_z\) の選択則は実験と直接比較できるようになります。ここでは、運動定数の中でも \(m_z\) と \(\sigma_z\) が対角となるハイゼンベルク表現をとります。 \(m_z\) の選択則では、\(m_z\) が \(\hbar\)、0、または \(-\hbar\) だけ変化することが求められますが、\(\sigma_z\) は電気変位と可換であるため、全く変化しません。したがって、遷移過程に関与する2つの状態間のエネルギー差は、磁場がない場合の値から \(e\mathscr{H}/2mc\)、0、または \(-e\hbar\mathscr{H}/2mc\) だけ異なります。 したがって、ボーアの周波数条件から、関連する電磁放射の周波数は、磁場がない場合の周波数から \(e\mathscr{H}/4\pi mc\)、0、または \(-e\mathscr{H}/4\pi mc\) だけ異なります。これは、磁場がない場合の各スペクトル線が磁場によって3つの成分に分割されることを意味します。\(z\)方向に移動する放射を考えると、(83)から、外側の2つの成分は円偏光になり、中心の変位していない成分は強度がゼロになります。これらの結果は、実験結果およびゼーマン効果の古典理論と一致しています。
